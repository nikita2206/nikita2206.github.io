## Design Context

### Users
Tech people, often arriving from Hacker News. They are looking for personal notes, short posts, and observations about tech and the industry without marketing language or productized self-branding.

### Brand Personality
Personal, plain, quiet. The site should feel like one person writing things down because they are worth keeping, not because they are trying to perform expertise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme. Super minimal. Geohot-style restraint is a useful reference: direct, sparse, and unconcerned with decorative polish. Typography should be quiet and unobtrusive rather than editorial, literary, or magazine-like. Avoid trendy flourishes, ornamental taglines, clever copy, and anything that reads like AI-generated refinement.

### Design Principles
1. Cut anything that sounds written for effect rather than clarity.
2. Let structure and spacing do the work; avoid decorative UI language.
3. Keep typography neutral, readable, and low-drama.
4. Prefer plain labels over brandy or editorial framing.
5. Make the site feel hand-made and personal, not optimized or overdesigned.
